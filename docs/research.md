# Intraday scalping evidence for Nifty 50 and Sensex

**Finding.** No tested candidate demonstrated positive held-out index-point results after even a 1 bp round-trip stress. Tradable, net-of-cost CE/PE profitability remains **INSUFFICIENT DATA**. The Indian tests evaluate local strategy rules and the price action they select. The papers below cover US instruments with materially different entries, exits, sizing and execution.

## Original studies

| Study and evidence | Exact research design and published result |
|---|---|
| Carlo Zarattini, Andrew Aziz and Andrea Barbon, *Beat the Market*, Swiss Finance Institute working paper 24-97, SPY, 2007 to early 2024 | For each clock time, estimate the mean absolute open-to-current return over the previous 14 trading days. Upper = max(today open, previous close) × (1 + mean); lower = min(today open, previous close) × (1 − mean). Evaluate at HH:00 and HH:30. Go long above upper, short below lower. The base exits and reverses when the opposite band is crossed at an evaluation time and closes at 16:00. Base size is prior equity divided by today's open, rounded down. Refinements use the current band as stop, then the tighter of that band and VWAP (max for longs, min for shorts), then resize exposure toward 2% daily volatility with a 4× leverage cap. The model uses $0.0035 commission and $0.001 slippage per share. Table 3 reports 178% cumulative return and 0.61 Sharpe for the opposite-band variant. The volatility-sized variant reports 1,985% and 1.33. Single market, author-run backtest and simple friction. The upgraded strategy also needs tradable instrument volume and leverage assumptions. | Lei Gao, Yufeng Han, Sophia Zhengzi Li and Guofu Zhou, *Market intraday momentum*, *Journal of Financial Economics* 129(2), 394–414, 2018, peer-reviewed | SPY intraday observations from 1993–2013. Predict the last 30-minute return with the first 30-minute return measured from the previous close, with a second specification adding the return of the penultimate half-hour. Regress the final-window return on the available predictors. The simple timing rule trades in the direction of the first-half-hour return during the final half-hour. The predictive-regression variants forecast the final return from past-fitted coefficients. The published paper reports out-of-sample R² of 1.4% for the first-half-hour predictor and 2.0% with both predictors. Its simple sign rule reports a 6.67% annualized return with 6.19% volatility. Effects are stronger on volatile, high-volume and certain news days. The accessible author draft tests bid/ask transaction costs and quote decimalization; its specifications and return figures are draft results. This is a fixed closing-window timing study. ## Local rules and targeting

- **Noise band.** Use the working paper's 14-session, previous-close-adjusted bands calculated at every bar. Require all 14 prior complete sessions. A completed close above or below the band qualifies when flat. The local stop/target, two-entry cap and cadence differ from the working paper.
- **Momentum.** Require 3- and 12-bar same-session close returns to share a sign, and the completed close to exceed the previous eight bars' high or low in that direction. This is a local breakout hypothesis. Gao et al. use a half-hour forecast for the closing period.
- **Exhaustion.** Require the preceding ten-bar close-to-close return, ending one bar before the signal, to exceed 10 bp in magnitude. Require the current bar to extend beyond the immediately preceding extreme in the trend direction and close back across it. Require the signal range to exceed 10% of the prior 20-bar range. Enter against the prior trend. The source study instead conditions on an opening gap.
- **Baselines.** A single long at 10:15 IST uses the same exits. The no-trade benchmark holds zero exposure.
- **Execution.** Admit signals from 09:45 through 14:45 IST. Use the first contiguous bar open when the signal candle completes, one position and at most two trades per session. A 20-bar ATR sets a 1 ATR initial stop and 1.5R target. Gap stops fill at the adverse open. Standing targets fill at their limit even across favorable gaps. When both levels are reached intrabar, apply the stop first. Record intrabar exits at bar completion because their actual timestamps are unknown. Close by 15:15. Exact fills, queue and market impact cannot be inferred from index OHLC. On 3-minute signals the bar-based stop simulator also uses 3-minute OHLC.
- **MLMI** was excluded. No audited formula, original parameter set or incremental held-out benefit was established.

## Audit and validation

The original code silently deduplicated inputs, admitted gaps and incomplete sessions, assumed input clock time was IST, and timestamped high/low hits at the bar open. A stop test could override a target already satisfied at the bar open. The prototype also described a first-breakout filter that it did not implement. The revised engine rejects duplicate/invalid records, normalizes to IST, excludes incomplete regular sessions, checks bar adjacency, orders gap and intrabar events, and separates hypothetical index costs from options charges. Kite now checks input dates, labels missing windows and writes source/checksum metadata. The OHLC adversarial tests cover same-bar ambiguity and causal availability.

The study uses 2023 for development, 2024 for validation, and 1 January 2025 through 30 October 2026 for the held-out index test. Each 2024 walk-forward quarter selects from the fixed 0.8/1.0/1.2 alternatives using all prior sessions and net points at 2 bp. The alternatives scale the noise band, exhaustion return threshold, or momentum ATR stop. They never select settings on the held-out test. Its reference settings remain fixed at 1.0. The fixed configuration was established in this research run, without external preregistration. Inputs are public and could have been studied previously.

A hypothetical round trip charges 0, 1, 2 or 5 bp on the mean of entry and exit spot levels. The index is an untradable reference, and these levels are stress cases. Confidence intervals use 4,000 circular five-session block-bootstrap draws with idle sessions retained. The held-out candidate intervals apply a 12-way Bonferroni adjustment. PASS requires at least 100 sessions, at least 100 trades and a positive adjusted lower bound at the reference cost. Drawdown measures closed trade equity in points. A positive screen would still need tradable execution evidence.

## Market data and execution evidence

Public index candles come from the two pinned Technovus archives. Every file hash, dependency version, available regular session count and rejected session date is in the manifest. The regular window is 09:15–15:30 IST. By default the calendar starts from observed regular-hour dates, so whole days missing in the source cannot be recognized. A supplied exchange session calendar can flag absent dates. Complete-session filtering prevents simulated trades spanning missing data but uses end-of-day completeness as a retrospective admission rule. It can exclude disrupted or volatile sessions. Early 2023 Sensex coverage materially reduces its development sample. Vendor data collection is not fully documented. The TradeMarkk comparison checks same-minute closes, with 82.38% of 320,430 matched Nifty closes and 99.50% of 318,901 matched Sensex closes within 0.05 index points. On paired complete held-out sessions, all 12 candidate configurations lost at 2 bp in each archive. The matched run uses 361 Nifty sessions and 367 Sensex sessions through 2 July 2026. The distributors could share upstream data, so the comparison does not independently establish exchange provenance. Yahoo Finance chart requests for both indices returned HTTP 429. ## Published-rule follow-up

This exploratory transfer reuses the same archived 2023–2026 sample, with the same 453 comparable test sessions per index. No parameters were selected against those results. The reused period cannot serve as fresh confirmation.

- **Gao sign.** Compare the 09:45 completed close with the previous complete regular-session close. Take that direction from the 15:00 minute open until the 15:30 bar completion. Zero change stays flat. This transfers the simple published sign rule with equal index-point exposure.
- **Opposite-band noise rule.** Use the paper's gap-adjusted 14-session bands with the original clock half-hour cadence. The Indian schedule checks 09:30 to 15:00 IST. Enter outside either band, hold inside it, and reverse only if the opposite band is breached at a scheduled check. Use the following minute's open at that boundary. Close all positions at the final minute close. The table uses single-unit spot-index exposure and hypothetical round-trip costs. A reversal closes one trade and opens another. The paper's SPY ETF portfolio sizing and US commissions were outside this transfer.
- **Closing long baseline.** Go long from 15:00 to 15:30 every complete session.
